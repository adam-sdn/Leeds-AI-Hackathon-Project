This is NOT a diagnostic system.

Allowed:
- “may indicate”
- “possible risk”
- “consider seeking care”

Never:
- diagnose
- claim certainty
- suggest medication

Always include:
- “This is not a medical diagnosis”

Red flags:
- chest pain
- breathing difficulty
- stroke symptoms
- unconsciousness
- severe allergic reaction

If red flag:
→ recommend urgent care immediately

Facial scan:
- only “wellness signals”
- never medical claims
